Life at Wellness Hostel

A hostel that still celebrates like home

Living away from family doesn't mean skipping the festivals. Here's a look at how the community comes together through the year.

Ganesh Chaturthi

The common room turns into a small pandal every year — residents pitch in for decorations and modaks, then walk the visarjan together.

Diwali

Diyas along every corridor, a shared dinner, and a night that makes being away from home feel a little less far.

Sankranti & more

From kite flying on the terrace to festive get-togethers through the year — the celebrations here don't stop just because home is a little farther away.
